How to open HEIC on Windows 11 / Windows 10

Windows doesn't include HEIC support out of the box — you'll see "Can't open this file" or a blank thumbnail. You have three options:

Option A: Free browser converter (recommended)

Use the HEIC to JPG converter above. No install, no cost, works on any Windows version. Batch convert unlimited photos.

Option B: Install HEIF Image Extensions

Microsoft offers an official codec on the Microsoft Store:

  1. Open Microsoft Store, search for "HEIF Image Extensions".
  2. Install it (costs $0.99).
  3. Restart Photos app — HEIC thumbnails and preview should now work.

Note: there's also a free "HEVC Video Extensions from Device Manufacturer" hidden in the Store, but Microsoft keeps moving the link. The $0.99 one is more reliable.

Option C: CopyTrans HEIC for Windows

Free third-party codec that adds HEIC thumbnails to Windows Explorer. Download from copytrans.net. Works on Windows 7–11.

How to open HEIC on macOS

macOS has native HEIC support since High Sierra (10.13). Just double-click to open in Preview. To convert:

  1. Right-click the HEIC file in Finder.
  2. Select Quick Actions → Convert Image.
  3. Choose JPG or PNG and the desired size.

For batch conversion on Mac, use the web converter — faster than doing them one by one in Finder.

How to view HEIC on iPhone / iPad

iPhones and iPads open HEIC natively in the Photos app — no extra steps needed. If you're sending HEIC photos to someone without an Apple device:

  1. Option 1: Go to Settings → Photos, scroll to "Transfer to Mac or PC", and select Automatic. When you email or share, iOS will auto-convert to JPEG.
  2. Option 2: Before taking photos, go to Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible. This saves new photos as JPEG instead of HEIC.
  3. Option 3: Use the HEIC to JPG converter in Safari on your iPhone. It works in mobile browsers too.

How to open HEIC on Android

Android 10+ supports HEIC natively (Google Photos, Files by Google). For older Android versions:

  1. Open Chrome and go to HEIC Tools.
  2. Tap the dropzone, pick your HEIC files from the file picker.
  3. Convert to JPG — then share or save the result.

Alternatively, install "HEIC Converter" from Google Play (many ad-supported options exist).

Why does my iPhone shoot HEIC anyway?

Apple switched to HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) with iOS 11 because:

  • HEIC files are ~50% smaller than JPEG at the same quality.
  • It supports 16-bit color depth (JPEG is 8-bit).
  • One HEIC container can hold multiple images (burst, HDR stacks).

The tradeoff: compatibility. Most non-Apple apps and websites expect JPEG or PNG. That's why a converter is essential.

Common HEIC problems (and fixes)

"Can't open this file" on Windows
Windows lacks native HEIC support. Install the HEIF Image Extensions from Microsoft Store, or use the free browser converter above.
HEIC photos look washed out when converted
This happens with HDR (gain map) HEIC photos. Try converting to PNG instead of JPG, which preserves the full color range.
HEIC to email attachment shows as .heic not .jpg
Go to Settings → Photos → "Transfer to Mac or PC" → Automatic. Otherwise, convert before attaching.
