FAQ

Why convert PNG to JPG?
PNG files are often 5-10× larger than JPG for photos. Converting to JPG saves storage space and makes sharing faster. JPG also has better compatibility.
Are my photos uploaded?
No. Everything runs in your browser using the Canvas API. Files never leave your device.
Will I lose transparency?
Yes — JPG doesn't support transparency. Transparent areas will be filled with white. If you need transparency, keep the PNG or convert to WebP instead.
