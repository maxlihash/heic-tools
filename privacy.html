Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

TL;DR

Your photos never leave your device. Conversion is 100% local. We use minimal analytics and advertising cookies to keep the site free.

What we don't do

  • No file upload: all HEIC decoding runs locally in your browser with WebAssembly. Your photos stay on your device.
  • No personal data collection: we don't ask for your name, email, or any account information.

Analytics & advertising

To keep the site free and unlimited, we use:

  • Google Analytics: anonymous usage stats (pages visited, browser type). No personal data.
  • Microsoft Clarity: anonymized UX insights (clicks, scroll depth). No keystrokes or personal data.
  • Google AdSense: serves ads; may use cookies for frequency capping and interest-based advertising. See Google's ad privacy policy.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. The converter works without cookies.

What our host may log

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may log anonymous infrastructure data (e.g. request counts, bandwidth). This is standard for any site hosted on Cloudflare. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Third-party CDN

We load heic2any and JSZip from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). jsDelivr may log anonymous request data per their privacy policy. The conversion code runs locally in your browser — the CDN only delivers the JavaScript library.
